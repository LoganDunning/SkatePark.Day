Best Skate Day

01 — About

Find the Best Skate Park Day This Week

best.skatepark.day checks the 7-day weather forecast for your location and picks the best skate park day of the week, which is the day with the best conditions for skateboarding at your local skatepark.

02 — Method

How We Pick the Best Day to Skate

  • Temperature: warm days score highest, and anything over 30°C loses points.
  • Wind speed: we check hourly wind, and gusts above 15 km/h rule a session out.
  • Rain: wet ramps and bowls aren't skateable, so dry days come first.
  • Sun, clouds and UV: plenty of sun with low UV is ideal.

03 — FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best skate park day?

It's the day in the next seven with the best mix of warm temperature, low wind, no rain and comfortable sun and UV for skating outdoors.

Is best.skatepark.day free?

Yes. It's free, needs no account, and works in any browser on desktop or mobile.

How does it know where I am?

It asks for your location. If you say no, it estimates your area from your IP address, and you can also search for any city.

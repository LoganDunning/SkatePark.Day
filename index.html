01 — About

Find the Best Skate Park Day This Week

best.skatepark.day checks the 7-day weather forecast for your location and picks the best skate park day of the week, which is the day with the best conditions for skateboarding at your local skatepark.

Looking for a park? Browse 333 Ontario skate parks by city, each with this week's skate forecast and a calendar feed, for example Toronto, Ottawa, London and Mississauga.

02 — Method

How We Pick the Best Day to Skate

  • Temperature: warm days score highest, and anything over 30°C loses points.
  • Wind speed: we check hourly wind, and gusts above 15 km/h rule a session out.
  • Rain: wet ramps and bowls aren't skateable, so dry days come first.
  • Sun, clouds and UV: plenty of sun with low UV is ideal.

03 — FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best skate park day?

It's the day in the next seven with the best mix of warm temperature, low wind, no rain and comfortable sun and UV for skating outdoors.

Is best.skatepark.day free?

Yes. It's free, needs no account, and works in any browser on desktop or mobile.

How does it know where I am?

It asks for your location. If you say no, it starts with Toronto, and you can search for any city or town.

What's the best weather for skateboarding?

Dry ground, light wind under about 10 km/h and temperatures between 18°C and 25°C. Cool, dry days still score well; any rain in a two-hour window rules it out.

How windy is too windy to skate?

Once hourly wind passes 15 km/h, a two-hour window scores zero here. Gusts push boards around on ramps and make flip tricks unpredictable.

Can you skate after it rains?

Usually not right away. Wet concrete and wooden ramps are slippery and soak bearings, so the forecast puts dry days and dry two-hour windows first.

Does it cover skateparks in Ontario?

Yes. It knows 333 skateparks across Ontario and works for any city or town worldwide. Each Ontario park has its own calendar feed with the best skate time every week. See every Ontario skate park by city.

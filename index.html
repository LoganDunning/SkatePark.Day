🏆 Best Skate Day

How we find the best skate park day

best.skatepark.day checks the 7-day weather forecast for your location and ranks every day to find the best skate park day of the week. Temperature and wind speed matter most, followed by sun and UV.

  • Temperature: warm but not over 30°C (86°F).
  • Wind: hourly wind under 15 km/h. Anything above that is a write-off.
  • Rain: dry days only. Wet ramps and bowls aren't safe.
  • Sun & UV: plenty of sun with low UV and low cloud.

It's free, works on any phone or computer, and needs no sign-up. Allow location access or search for a city to get the best day to skate near you.
